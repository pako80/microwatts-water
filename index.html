What is MicroWatTS?

Micro WatTS shall develop a micro greywater solar water treatment system — a step in the right direction when it comes to tackling the problem of water shortage that islands such as Malta and Sicily face.

Photocatalytic Surfaces

Research into heterogeneous photocatalysis to identify polymers and surfaces with photocatalytic potential for effective greywater decontamination.

Solar Treatment

Standalone micro solar water treatment systems suitable for deployment in typical domestic residences or small-scale industrial concerns.

Cross-Border Collaboration

A partnership of universities, research institutions and SMEs from Malta and Sicily working together on innovative eco-products and technology transfer.

The Partners

MicroWatTS is headed by the University of Malta in collaboration with leading research institutions and industry partners.
